Privacy-first principles engineered for Google OAuth Verification

{# Trust 1: Sign-in #}

Google Sign-In

Google Sign-In is used exclusively to authenticate household identity. Vault receives your verified name, email address, and profile picture. No external passwords are ever stored.

{# Trust 2: Gmail (read-only) #}
mail

Gmail (Read-Only, Optional)

When connected, Vault queries your inbox strictly for bank alerts and credit card statements. Only parsed transaction metadata is saved; full email bodies are never stored on disk.

{# Trust 3: Never Shared #}

Never Shared or Sold

All financial records stay on your private deployment. Access tokens are encrypted at rest with Fernet symmetric cryptography, and you can revoke integration access at any time.

Vault is invite-only: deployment and access are restricted exclusively to members of a single household. Complete regulatory terms and data rights are detailed in our Privacy Policy.
