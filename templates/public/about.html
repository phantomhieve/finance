Vault is a private personal finance dashboard for a single family. It tracks savings, investments, rent and jewellery, and automatically records card and bank spending from transaction alert emails, so the household always knows where its money goes.

Sign in with Google How we handle your data

What Vault does

📈

Portfolio tracking

Mutual funds, stocks and fixed deposits with returns, allocation and AI-generated insights.

💳

Expense tracker

Reads bank and credit card alert emails (read-only) to log spends, categorise them and flag unusual charges.

🧾

Card statements

Matches statement PDFs against alerts, adds missed transactions and reminds you before bills are due.

🏠

Savings & HRA

Financial-year savings, rent paid and tax-relevant records in one place.

How Vault uses Google data

🔑

Sign-in

Google Sign-In is used only to verify your identity. Vault receives your name, email and profile picture.

✉️

Gmail (read-only, optional)

If you connect Gmail, Vault searches for transaction alerts and statements and stores only the parsed amount, merchant, card and date. Email bodies are never stored.

🛡️

Never shared or sold

Data stays on Vault's own server, access tokens are encrypted, and you can disconnect at any time.

Vault is invite-only: access is limited to members of one household. Full details are in the Privacy Policy.
