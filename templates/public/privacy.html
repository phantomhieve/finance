Privacy Policy

Last updated: {{ updated }}

Vault ("we", "the app"), available at {{ site_url }}, is a private personal finance dashboard used by the members of one household. This policy explains what data Vault collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

1. Data we collect

  • Google account profile (via Google Sign-In): your name, email address and profile picture, used to identify you and control access.
  • Financial records you enter: savings, investments, rent, jewellery and similar entries you add yourself.
  • Gmail data (optional, read-only): only if you choose to connect your Gmail inbox. See section 2.
  • Statement PDF passwords you enter so card statements can be opened. These are stored encrypted.
  • Technical data: standard server logs (IP address, browser, timestamps) used for security and troubleshooting.

2. How Vault uses Gmail data

When you connect Gmail, Vault requests the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly scope and uses it only to:

  • Search your inbox for bank and credit card transaction alerts, credit card statements and bill payment confirmations.
  • Extract the transaction amount, merchant, card (last digits), date and currency from those emails.
  • Download credit card statement PDF attachments to reconcile transactions and find due dates.

Vault does not store full email bodies. It keeps only the parsed transaction fields and a masked subject line for reference. Vault never sends, modifies or deletes your email.

3. Use of AI services

When an alert email isn't in a format Vault recognises, or a merchant needs a category, the relevant text snippet is sent to Google's Gemini API to extract the transaction or suggest a category. It is not used to build advertising profiles.

4. Google API Services User Data Policy

Vault's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. In particular:

  • Google user data is used only to provide the features described above, which are visible in the app.
  • It is not transferred to third parties, except as needed to provide those features (such as the AI processing in section 3), to comply with law, or with your consent.
  • It is never used for advertising, never sold, and never used to determine creditworthiness or for lending.
  • No human reads your Gmail data unless you give explicit permission (for example, to debug a problem you report), it is needed for security or legal reasons, or it has been aggregated and anonymised.

5. Storage and security

  • Data is stored in a database on Vault's own server and is served only over HTTPS.
  • Gmail refresh tokens and statement passwords are encrypted at rest with symmetric (Fernet) encryption.
  • Access is restricted to approved household members who sign in with Google. Optional biometric unlock is supported.
  • Encrypted database backups are kept in private Google Cloud Storage.

6. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your personal data. Household members you have been grouped with can see shared household figures (for example, combined monthly spend), as configured in the app.

7. Retention and deletion

  • You can disconnect Gmail at any time from Expenses → Settings. This deletes the stored token, and Vault stops reading your inbox immediately.
  • You can also revoke Vault's access from your Google Account permissions page.
  • Parsed transactions are kept until you delete them or ask for your account to be deleted. To delete your account and all associated data, email us at the address below; we will do so within 30 days.

8. Children

Vault is not intended for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above. Material changes will be communicated to users.
