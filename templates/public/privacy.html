Vault (“we”, “the app”), deployed at {{ site_url }}, is an invite-only personal finance and wealth intelligence dashboard operated strictly for members of a single household. This policy explains what data Vault collects, how it is used, and the absolute controls you retain over your information.

1. Data we collect

  • Google account profile (via Google Sign-In): your name, email address, and profile picture, used exclusively to identify you and control household access.
  • Financial records you enter: savings entries, investment balances, rent records, jewellery catalog items, and financial notes you manually enter or import.
  • Gmail data (optional, read-only): only if you explicitly choose to connect your Gmail inbox. See section 2 below.
  • Statement PDF passwords you enter so credit card statements can be decrypted locally. These are stored encrypted at rest.
  • Technical data: standard server logs (IP address, browser user-agent, timestamps) used solely for operational security and troubleshooting.

2. How Vault uses Gmail data

When you connect Gmail, Vault requests the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly scope and uses it exclusively to:

  • Search your inbox for automated bank and credit card transaction alerts, credit card statements, and bill payment confirmations.
  • Extract the transaction amount, merchant name, card last-4 digits, transaction date, and currency from those specific alert emails.
  • Download credit card statement PDF attachments to reconcile transactions and compute billing due dates.
Email Body Privacy Commitment: Vault does not store full email bodies. It keeps only the structured, parsed transaction metadata and a masked subject line for user verification. Vault never sends, modifies, or deletes your email.

3. Use of AI services

When an incoming alert email is not in a structured format recognized by local parsers, or when a merchant name requires categorization, the relevant text snippet is sent to Google’s Gemini API to extract transaction metadata or suggest an expense category. This data is never used to train public models or build advertising profiles.

4. Google API Services User Data Policy

Vault’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Specifically:

  • Google user data is used only to provide user-facing features described above and visible directly within the application interface.
  • Data is not transferred to third parties, except as strictly necessary to provide those features (such as AI extraction in section 3), to comply with applicable law, or with your explicit consent.
  • It is never used for advertising, never sold, and never used to determine creditworthiness or for consumer lending.
  • No humans read your Gmail data unless you provide explicit permission (e.g., to debug a specific parsing error), it is legally required for security or regulatory reasons, or it has been aggregated and anonymized.

5. Storage and security

  • All application data is stored in a private database on Vault’s dedicated server, served exclusively over encrypted HTTPS with TLS 1.3.
  • Gmail OAuth refresh tokens and statement PDF passwords are encrypted at rest using symmetric (Fernet / AES-128-CBC) encryption keys.
  • Access is strictly restricted to approved household members authenticated via Google OAuth 2.0. Optional hardware biometric unlock (WebAuthn passkeys) is supported.
  • Database backups are encrypted before storage in private, customer-managed Google Cloud Storage buckets.

6. Sharing

We do not sell, rent, monetize, or publicly share your personal data. Within the application, household members grouped into your AccountGroup can view shared household metrics (such as aggregate monthly spends and physical jewellery custody), subject to configured role permissions.

7. Retention and deletion

  • You can disconnect Gmail at any time from Expenses → Settings. This immediately deletes the stored refresh token, and Vault stops accessing your inbox.
  • You can also revoke Vault’s access directly from your Google Account Permissions page.
  • Parsed transaction records are retained until you delete them or request complete household account erasure. To request full account deletion, email us at the address below; we will permanently erase all associated records within 30 days.

8. Children

Vault is engineered for adult household financial administration and does not knowingly collect or solicit data from children under 13.

9. Changes

If our data practices evolve, we will update the “Last updated” date at the top of this policy. Material policy changes will be highlighted directly to users upon signing in.

10. Contact

For questions, privacy inquiries, or data erasure requests, contact the administrator directly at: {{ contact_email }}
